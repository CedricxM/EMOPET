Do not assign fake probabilities or fake precision.

- decision leverage;
- next evidence path;
- cost to reduce, or `UNKNOWN`;
- time to reduce, or `UNKNOWN`;
- result when measured;
- resulting decision.

**Hidden unknowns are more dangerous than visible unknowns.**
