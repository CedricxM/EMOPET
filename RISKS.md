## Control principle

A risk is not closed because:

- a document exists;
- a PR merged;
- a supplier answered;
- an investor showed interest;
- a workaround exists.

Closure requires the evidence and authority appropriate to the underlying domain.

No risk probability is assigned merely because a dashboard wants a red/amber/green number.
