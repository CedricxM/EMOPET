# EMOPET — Company Control Plane

> **Status:** company-level navigation and state index.  
> **It does not replace controlled domain authorities.**

EMOPET is using GitHub as the control plane for the whole project/company, not only for code.

Start here when the question is: **what is true now, what controls that truth, what remains open, and what proof is required next?**

## Read order

1. `AI_READ_FIRST.md` — mandatory context gate.

4. `CORPORATE.md` — public-safe corporate/IP projection.

6. `FRESHNESS.md` — review freshness and STALE semantics.

8. `docs/company/EMOPET_COMPANY_OS_ARCHITECTURE_2026-10-01.md` — operating model and source-of-truth hierarchy.
9. Relevant controlled domain authority before making a substantive decision.

## Truth model

**STATE** says what the current projection is.

**EVIDENCE** says how we know.

**HISTORY** says how we got here and what was superseded.

The controlling source always wins over the projection. GitHub may index evidence that remains in Gmail, legal archives, supplier systems or other controlled stores.

## Non-negotiable rule

A polished dashboard is not maturity.

No Company OS view may turn:

- proposed into decided;
- implemented into validated;
- sent into received;
- discussion into partnership;
- synthetic tests into physical proof;
- supplier work into EMOPET release authority;
- public article access into commercial rights;
- a funding conversation into eligibility, award or committed capital.

The job of this layer is to make uncertainty visible enough that EMOPET can retire it deliberately.
