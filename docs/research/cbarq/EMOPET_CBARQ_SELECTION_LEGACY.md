# EMOPET C-BARQ Selection Proposal — Historical Design Record

**Status:** SUPERSEDED / FOR TRACEABILITY ONLY  
**Original source:** `EMOPET_CBARQ_Selection-JS eds.pdf`  
**Prepared for:** Prof. James A. Serpell  
**Author:** Cédric Mian — EMOPET, Lorient, France

## Why this file exists

This document preserves the earlier EMOPET behavioural-design direction in which a limited set of C-BARQ subscales was being considered for the ELI. It is retained so that the evolution of the scientific design remains auditable.

It is **not the current implementation specification**.

The current direction is to seek the appropriate licence for the **complete validated C-BARQ instrument** and to work with qualified scientific partners on how it may be integrated into the EMOPET experience without compromising validity.

## Earlier proposal

The earlier proposal treated questionnaire burden as a constraint and targeted approximately twenty items drawn from three complete behavioural constructs:

- **Separation-related behaviour** — intended to initialise an owner-absent sub-baseline and separation/anticipation context.
- **Excitability** — intended to calibrate an arousal-reactivity prior and reduce false positives.
- **Non-social fear** — intended to contextualise arousal changes associated with noise, novelty and unfamiliar environments.

A short activity/energy construct was considered as an optional addition if the item budget allowed it.

## Earlier scientific questions

The proposal asked Prof. Serpell whether:

1. those three constructs were the right priorities for an inference engine;
2. stranger-directed or dog-directed fear should replace one of them for social disambiguation; and
3. using a subset of complete subscales would be psychometrically acceptable compared with a validated short form.

## Important change in direction

The project no longer assumes that questionnaire burden should be solved by deleting or selecting items.

The design question is now whether the **complete instrument** can be made part of the product experience in a scientifically defensible way, potentially by pacing questions over time. That possibility is **not considered validated** and must be reviewed with the instrument owners / scientific experts before implementation.

The product must adapt to the science, not quietly alter the measurement conditions for UX convenience.

## Treatment of the illustrative item draft

The original PDF included EMOPET-written illustrative questions. Those questions were explicitly not C-BARQ items and were intended only to make the approximate scope tangible.

They are not an approved replacement instrument and must not be shipped, scored or represented as C-BARQ.

## Repository rule

Any implementation that uses the name **C-BARQ** must refer to the licensed, validated instrument or to clearly labelled scientific metadata about it. Breed heuristics, onboarding approximations and EMOPET-authored behavioural questions must not be labelled "C-BARQ simplified".
