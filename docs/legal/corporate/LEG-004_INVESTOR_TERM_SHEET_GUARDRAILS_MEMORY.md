## 1. Economics

Target the smallest dilution compatible with the milestone financed.

Valuation should be justified by evidence, not urgency.

Before signing, show the fully diluted cap table.

### Liquidation preference — preferred negotiation position

- 1x;
- non-participating;
- no cumulative dividend unless explicitly justified.

### Anti-dilution

Preferred if required: broad-based weighted-average.

Avoid full ratchet except extraordinary negotiated circumstances.

### Option/BSPCE pool

Always specify:

- pre-money or post-money;
- exact percentage;
- who bears dilution;
- refresh mechanics.

## 2. Governance

### Board / strategic committee

Initial preference:

- founder retains appointment/control appropriate to ownership stage;
- investor observation rights can precede a voting board seat;
- independent member only through a mutually approved process.

### Reserved matters

Limit investor consent to exceptional corporate-protection matters.

Do **not** give investor vetoes over ordinary operating matters such as:

- product-feature approval;
- ordinary supplier approval;
- ordinary hiring;
- routine pricing;
- day-to-day technical decisions.

## 3. Transfer rights

### Tag-along

Reasonable minority protection.

### Drag-along

Require:

- high approval threshold;
- minimum protections on consideration;
- identical economic treatment for equivalent securities;
- no disproportionate founder warranties;
- founder consent while an agreed founder-control threshold is maintained.

### Founder Permitted Transfers

A carefully drafted category should allow appropriate internal founder-controlled transfers where:

- founder remains the ultimate controlling person;
- receiving entity accedes to the shareholders' agreement;
- ordinary pre-emption/tag/drag mechanics are not triggered solely because of the internal transfer.

Exact drafting is for counsel.

## 4. Founder leaver clauses

Avoid subjective or overbroad bad-leaver triggers.

Require where appropriate:

- objective definitions;
- cure process;
- fair valuation rules;
- credit for pre-investment service;
- no automatic confiscation of vested economic rights for minor breaches.

## 5. Intellectual property

Investment documents should confirm:

- the EMOPET operating company owns/controls the IP required for the business;
- no investor receives direct ownership of core operating IP merely by investing;
- no investor receives an implied licence;
- strategic licences require separate written agreements;
- disposal of core IP remains a reserved matter.

## 6. Strategic investors

Any commercial right granted alongside investment must be separately scoped by factors such as:

- product;
- territory;
- duration;
- field of use;
- minimum performance;
- termination.

No automatic right of first refusal over a future company sale unless separately negotiated and valued.

## 7. No-sign checklist

Do not sign until at least:

- cap table before/after is simulated;
- voting power before/after is simulated;
- next financing is simulated;
- liquidation waterfall is simulated;
- option-pool dilution is simulated;
- conversion instruments are included;
- veto list is line-reviewed;
- IP clauses are line-reviewed;
- drag/tag/pre-emption clauses are line-reviewed;
- counsel confirms the interaction between articles and shareholders' agreement.

## 8. Boundary

This file preserves founder negotiation doctrine. It is not a term sheet, investment offer, shareholders' agreement or substitute for legal advice.
