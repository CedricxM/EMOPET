## Status vocabulary

- `DRAFT`: text being prepared or reviewed.
- `PREPARED_FOR_SIGNATURE`: nominative instrument ready to be completed/signed.
- `SENT_FOR_SIGNATURE`: instrument delivered to the intended signer; signature not yet evidenced.
- `SIGNED`: signed copy received and archived.
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced by a later controlled instrument.

## Evidence boundary

The editable/source text is versioned in this private repository. Completed signature copies may contain postal addresses, private emails and signatures and should **not** be committed automatically. Store executed originals in the controlled legal archive and record only the execution metadata here unless a specific legal-retention decision authorizes repository storage.

For each executed agreement, record:

- effective date;
- date signed by each Party;
- archive location / document ID;
- SHA-256 of the executed file;
- any amendment or annex reference.

## Related instruments

Confidentiality does not determine ownership of contributor-created deliverables. Separate contribution / IP-rights instruments and deliverables schedules are used for that purpose.

## Next update gate

Update this register when each signer returns a completed agreement. Do not change a status to `SIGNED` based on a verbal confirmation or a sent-message screenshot alone.
