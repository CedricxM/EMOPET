# EMOPET — WORLD ZERO-CHAT COOPERATION TEST PROTOCOL v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**GitHub:** #46 / linked #49  
**Status:** PROPOSED PLAYTEST PROTOCOL / ZERO RESULTS CLAIMED

## 1. Research question

Can two Guardians who do not know each other complete `La piste commune` and experience comfortable social connection using only:
- spatial presence;
- human-avatar emotes;
- Quiet Social Layer presets;
- shared objectives?

## 2. Test conditions

### Condition A — Quiet only
- no free-text chat;
- presets + human emotes only.

### Condition B — Quiet + free text
- presets + human emotes;
- free-text chat where safety policy permits.

## 3. Participants

Design target for first controlled study:
- pairs of participants;
- some pairs unfamiliar with each other;
- hardware ownership not required.

No claim that this sample is statistically representative.

## 4. Scenario

1. Participant A and B enter Hub.
2. They see each other as `STRANGER`.
3. One sends `Salut`.
4. One invites the other to `La piste commune`.
5. They enter activity.
6. They must find markers.
7. They must coordinate the split bridge.
8. They solve final route.
9. They return to Hub.
10. They may choose whether to send a connection request.

## 5. Observations

Record:
- time until first social action;
- invite accepted/declined;
- preset usage;
- repeated preset confusion;
- nonverbal coordination;
- moments of silence;
- stalled moments;
- accidental social pressure;
- whether one participant dominates;
- whether free text was necessary;
- spontaneous desire to reconnect;
- dog-avatar interpretation.

## 6. Post-test questions

Ask:
1. Did interacting with the other person feel natural?
2. Did you ever feel forced to respond?
3. Could you understand what the other person wanted?
4. Did the preset messages feel limiting or reassuring?
5. Would you want text chat immediately?
6. Did the shared activity feel more social than a normal feed?
7. Did you think the dog avatar was communicating your dog’s mood?
8. Would you interact with this person again?

## 7. Hard failure indicators

- users cannot coordinate without guessing;
- repeated preset spam;
- social interaction feels coercive;
- participants assume silence means rejection;
- one player can complete the task alone;
- dog avatar is read as emotional social feedback;
- block/leave is hard to find.

## 8. Evidence discipline

No claim of:
- social comfort;
- preference;
- retention;
- reduced harassment;
- accessibility;
- safety

until actual controlled test evidence exists.
