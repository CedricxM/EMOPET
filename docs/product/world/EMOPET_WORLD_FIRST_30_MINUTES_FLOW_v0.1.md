# EMOPET — WORLD FIRST 30 MINUTES EXPERIENCE FLOW v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED PLAYTEST SCRIPT / NOT IMPLEMENTED

## Design objective

The first 30 minutes must prove that World can create:
**presence → curiosity → contact → shared action → memory → personal attachment**

without:
- monetization;
- dog-health rewards;
- emotional dog-avatar expression;
- forced real-world meeting;
- long tutorial.

## Minute 0–3 — Arrival

The Guardian enters **Le Hub Bretagne**.

What they immediately see:
- their human avatar;
- their neutral dog avatar;
- one clear path to their personal space;
- one shared activity marker;
- one local discovery point;
- a small number of other opted-in Guardians if the test is multiplayer.

Breiz gives no lore dump.

Maximum onboarding message:
> “Bienvenue dans World. Ici, tu peux rencontrer d’autres Guardians, explorer et participer à des expériences ensemble. Ton espace t’attend juste là.”

Then control is returned to the player.

## Minute 3–6 — First agency

The player chooses one:
- walk to personal space;
- inspect discovery point;
- approach another Guardian;
- inspect activity marker.

No forced route.

The dog avatar follows using neutral locomotion.

## Minute 6–10 — Personal identity

If the player enters **Mon espace**:
- they place one starter object;
- choose one dog-avatar accessory/visual detail;
- view one empty “souvenir” slot;
- learn that shared activities can fill the space over time.

Nothing is purchased.

Nothing displays ELI or health status.

The objective is:
> “I already want to make this place mine.”

## Minute 10–13 — Social contact

Another Guardian is visible.

Possible interaction:
- human avatar wave;
- profile card;
- “Inviter à La piste commune”.

The invited player sees:
- who invited them;
- activity name;
- instance;
- accept/decline.

No real-world location is exchanged.

## Minute 13–23 — Cooperative activity: La piste commune

Two or more players enter a compact scene.

### Beat 1
Find the first environmental clue.

### Beat 2
A simple cooperative interaction requires two players to activate separate points.

### Beat 3
A source-backed regional discovery is revealed.

The source panel is available, but does not interrupt play unless opened.

### Beat 4
Players solve the final sequence/puzzle together.

Completion:
- no winner;
- no rank;
- each receives the same shared keepsake.

The dog avatars remain present, mobile and emotionally neutral.

## Minute 23–26 — Shared memory

Back in the hub:

A small card appears:
> “Vous avez terminé La piste commune ensemble.”

The player may:
- keep the participation memory private;
- place the keepsake in personal space;
- optionally send a friend request / connection request if that social model is later approved.

No automatic Community post.

## Minute 26–28 — Community connection

The user opens the related Community card/event.

They see:
- the moderated event/post;
- organizer;
- broad World region;
- comments/community layer;
- a clear separation from any EMOPET evidence surface.

If they create a public post, it enters `PENDING_MODERATION` rather than publishing instantly.

## Minute 28–30 — Return hook

Back in their personal space, the player places the keepsake.

A future-content placeholder shows:
> “D’autres découvertes arriveront dans ce territoire.”

No countdown pressure.
No streak.
No purchase button.

The experience ends with a natural question:
> “Would I come back to meet people or see what has changed here?”

## What the first 30 minutes must NOT contain

- ELI score;
- health notification;
- dog mood;
- shop;
- premium prompt;
- loot box;
- battle pass;
- daily streak;
- step count;
- leaderboard;
- “good Guardian” badge;
- emotional dog animation;
- exact real-world position;
- mandatory friend request;
- forced Community publication.
