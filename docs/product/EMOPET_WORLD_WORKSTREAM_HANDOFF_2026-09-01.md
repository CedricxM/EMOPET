## Locked direction for review

- World is an interactive persistent social experience, not a monetized game.
- Core experience: meeting, exploring, shared activities, persistent personal spaces, memories, events and regional discovery.
- No monetization layer is part of the current design direction.
- No dog-health/ELI/relationship scoring becomes gameplay.
- The real dog enriches representation but never becomes a game controller or source of competitive rewards.
- Dog avatar may animate but must remain emotionally non-expressive.
- Community and World remain separate connected surfaces.
- Public Community publishing uses a pre-publication moderation gate.
- Unity remains behind a kill gate.
- Nakama remains a realtime/session component, not durable truth authority.
