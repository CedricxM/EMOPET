## 1. Decision

The current visual authority for EMOPET is:

> **`EMOPET_Charte_Graphique_v2.html` — Charte graphique v2.0**

This authority governs the visual identity of EMOPET-owned documents, controlled publications, approved partner-confidential releases, product/UI expression, presentations and future document templates, subject to audience-specific disclosure rules.

This is a **brand authority decision only**. It does not alter Product, engineering, scientific, legal, regulatory or supplier decisions.

## 2. Why this authority is selected

The v2 charter is the most complete visual system in the controlled corpus and defines:

- the EMOPET wordmark and aperture mark;
- semantic rationale of the aperture;
- complete palette;
- typography roles;
- reliability-state visual language;
- iconography;
- document/brand tone;
- product components;
- extension logic;
- non-medical and uncertainty principles.

It explicitly connects brand language to EMOPET's product doctrine:

- instrument, not toy;
- non-medical;
- uncertainty made visible;
- no unsupported emotional labelling;
- Brittany/Lorient identity;
- species-agnostic extension.

### 3.3 Mark

The v2 aperture mark and EMOPET wordmark are the active mark system.

The aperture expresses:

- a measurement/gating perimeter;
- an explicit opening representing acknowledged uncertainty;
- a central measured instant.

The primary identity is species-agnostic. Do not introduce a paw/animal silhouette/muzzle as the primary identity without a later controlled brand decision.

## 4. Controlled-document publication profile

All EMOPET-owned controlled documents must remain recognisably EMOPET.

Professional does not mean visually anonymous.

General rules:

- restrained but visible EMOPET mark/wordmark;
- controlled typography/palette;
- clear document metadata;
- calm, technical, premium layout;
- strong whitespace;
- consistent headings/tables;
- document ID, revision, status, classification and page numbering;
- open/evidence-pending states remain visible;
- decorative treatment must never make an unresolved state appear closed.

Scientific/evidence documents should privilege citations, confidence, evidence maturity and limitations over decorative branding.

Partner-confidential/supplier documents may use the full EMOPET identity. Confidentiality controls the audience, not whether a document looks like EMOPET.

Third-party originals remain unchanged.

## 5. Superseded visual systems

### Historical Playfair/Montserrat/indigo-coral-teal system

Retained as **HISTORICAL / LEGACY BRAND MATERIAL**. Not current authority.

### BioRhyme / Inter / navy profile

Retained as **HISTORICAL / PROPOSED PUBLICATION PROFILE — SUPERSEDED AS CURRENT VISUAL AUTHORITY**.

It must not govern new documents unless a future controlled decision explicitly reintroduces a defined sub-profile.

### No silent hybridisation

No tool or contributor should silently mix the current v2 typography/palette with legacy systems as if they were co-equal authorities.

Historical artifacts remain traceable and should be transformed only through a controlled new revision, never rewritten in place.

## 6. Content authority remains separate

The visual charter does not govern technical truth.

Product, engineering, scientific, privacy, legal, supplier and manufacturing-release authorities remain separate.

> **A beautifully formatted document is not more mature because of its design.**

## 7. Mass-conversion gate

Before corpus-wide visual conversion, a pilot set should cover at least:

1. governance document;
2. Product/PRD document;
3. engineering decision record;
4. large technical table;
5. scientific/evidence document;
6. spreadsheet/register;
7. MOKO partner-confidential document.

Mass conversion requires content preservation, status rendering, typography/palette compliance, no clipping, correct metadata/page breaks, acceptable density and PDF page-by-page QA.
