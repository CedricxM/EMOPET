## Qualification rule

Before engagement, verify in writing:

- exact legal entity;
- point of contact;
- exact requested capability;
- applicable accreditation scope;
- equipment/method;
- deliverables/raw data;
- engineering ownership/IP;
- price;
- lead time;
- sample logistics;
- NDA;
- whether subcontractors are used.

No provider is marked `QUALIFIED` until those items are evidenced.
