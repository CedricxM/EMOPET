## Evidence rules

The register may only move through the following states:

- `CANDIDATE_NOT_CONTACTED`
- `OUTREACH_SENT`
- `RESPONSE_RECEIVED`
- `PILOT_SCOPE_DISCUSSION`
- `LETTER_OF_INTEREST_RECEIVED`
- `DECLINED`
- `DEFERRED`

A public website is evidence that an organisation exists and is active.

It is **not** evidence of:
- willingness to test EMOPET;
- partnership;
- endorsement;
- user recruitment approval;
- data-sharing permission.

## Outreach objective

The first contact should not ask clubs to “become partners” in the abstract.

The useful ask is narrower:

1. confirm interest in hearing about the future EMOPET prototype;
2. determine whether a small owner/dog pilot could be discussed once MAT/TAG are ready;
3. identify which activities/cohorts would be appropriate;
4. understand club constraints around recruitment, data collection and on-site testing;
5. ask whether the club would be willing to provide a non-binding letter of interest if the proposed scope is acceptable.

A letter of interest should still be represented as `LETTER_OF_INTEREST_RECEIVED`, not as a partnership.

## Data boundary

No personal contact details from public club pages are stored in the machine-readable register.

Only:
- organisation;
- territory;
- public activity evidence;
- possible role;
- source URLs;
- relationship status

are retained.
