# EMOPET Hardware Compliance — Official Authority References

Status: `P0 REFERENCE INDEX / VERIFY CURRENT CONSOLIDATED TEXT AT RELEASE`

Use official EU sources as the legal/reference authority. Standards editions and Official Journal harmonised-standard references must be re-checked when formal conformity work starts.

## Radio / cybersecurity

- Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU (RED): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53/oj/eng
- Current consolidated RED text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53
- Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/2191, harmonised standards for RED: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2022/2191
- Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/138, EN 18031 cybersecurity references and limitations/notices: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2025/138/oj/eng
- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/339, transition/repeal of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 with CRA full application: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/339/oj/eng

## Cyber Resilience Act

- Regulation (EU) 2024/2847: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng

Important timing recorded for P0: Article 14 reporting obligations apply from 11 September 2026; the Regulation generally applies from 11 December 2027. Always verify current corrigenda/amendments.

## Product safety

- General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj/eng

The GPSR/harmonisation-law interaction must be assessed against the final MAT/TAG classification rather than assuming every GPSR manufacturer provision stacks unchanged on top of RED.

## Batteries

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 concerning batteries and waste batteries: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj/eng

Article 11 removability/replaceability must be assessed against the final battery/product configuration and any current delegated derogations. The registry intentionally does not classify TAG into a derogation without evidence.

## Hazardous substances / end of life

- RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/65/oj/eng
- WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2012/19/oj/eng

## EMC

- EMC Directive 2014/30/EU: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj/eng

RED Article 3(1)(b) requires radio equipment to ensure an adequate level of electromagnetic compatibility as set out in the EMC framework. The final conformity route for each MAT/TAG configuration must be confirmed before test booking.

## Release rule

Links in this file are reference pointers, not a legal opinion and not proof of conformity. Final product classification, applicable requirements, standards, laboratory scope and declaration wording require production-intent hardware/firmware evidence and controlled compliance review.
