## 1. Audit method

This was not a keyword-only scan of the current repository.

The audit reconciled three layers:

1. **prior project-chat memory** — accepted founder decisions, constraints and repeated strategy discussions recovered from previous EMOPET conversations;
2. **ChatGPT Library artifacts** — controlled masters, audits, presentations, workstream handoffs, scientific/industrial records and historical lineage documents;
3. **current GitHub repository** — existing strategy, software, compliance, industrial, evidence and communication records.

The objective was not to copy every idea. The objective was to recover **decision-grade knowledge** and preserve its maturity/status.
