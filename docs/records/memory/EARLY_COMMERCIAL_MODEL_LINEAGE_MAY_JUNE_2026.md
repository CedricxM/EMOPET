# EMOPET — Early Commercial Model Lineage — May–June 2026

**Audit date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `RETROSPECTIVE CONTROLLED MEMORY / HISTORICAL HYPOTHESES`

## Purpose

Recover the origin of pricing, acquisition, veterinary-channel and data-commercialisation ideas without promoting old assumptions into current strategy.

## 1. Pricing origin recovered

The origin of the historical `€5.99/month` figure is now evidenced.

### 13 May — BMC v4

`EMOPET_BMC_AudeLor_v4.docx` described:

- `Breiz Free`: free application without hardware;
- kit + service subscription;
- hardware target below the psychological threshold of **€150**;
- Premium subscription target below **€6**;
- explicit `Breiz Premium (5,99 €/mois)`;
- local community as a central acquisition and retention mechanism.

Source: Gmail message `19e21097c83bee9a`.

### 29 May / 2 June — AudéLor commercial discussion

The commercial note embedded in Gmail message `19e888021d3ac2d6` says the project wanted to keep the subscription at **€5.99/month if possible**, partly because price complaints were seen as a friction in the connected-pet market.

This was paired with an early target segment and early market-size calculation. It was a planning hypothesis, not willingness-to-pay evidence.

### May financial/BOM workbook

`EMOPET_Couts.xlsx` modelled a two-tier historical offer:

- Essential: `€5.99/month`;
- Premium: `€8.99/month`, historically bundled with unlimited GPS + voice.

Because the TAG voice/GPS architecture later changed, that two-tier feature bundle is **SUPERSEDED AS PRODUCT SCOPE**.

## 2. Current pricing authority

The provenance gap is closed, but the commercial evidence gate remains open.

Current rules:

- `€5.99` is a **historical modelling hypothesis**, not a frozen price;
- willingness-to-pay remains unvalidated;
- hardware selling price remains open;
- current COGS require current quotes and evidence;
- privacy, consent, export/delete and fundamental trust controls are never paywalled;
- Premium scope must be derived from current product architecture, not the obsolete May TAG bundle.

## 3. Early acquisition doctrine

By May/June, the project already described a funnel broadly equivalent to:

`free/local experience → profile/community/Breiz engagement → perceived longitudinal value → MAT + TAG purchase → service subscription → local recommendation/retention`

The historical BMC treated community as a central pillar and Breiz as a local/regional companion rather than a generic chatbot.

This strategic lineage survives, but modern product doctrine rejects manipulative engagement optimisation.

## 4. Brittany-first is historical, not newly invented

The June 2 AudéLor email already described:

- Breiz knowing Brittany, local walks and cultural context;
- owner-to-owner local interaction;
- Brittany as the first territorial expression;
- later region-by-region adaptation.

The current decision that the launch/integration phase begins in **2027** is a timing refinement of this older regional strategy.

## 5. Veterinary-channel evolution

The May commercial note already treated veterinarians and relevant animal professionals as **prescribers rather than primary customers**.

Historical version:

- field salesperson targeting animal professionals;
- possible commission on kit/subscription sales.

Current version:

- Guardian/consumer remains final customer;
- veterinary professional is a prescriber, credibility/feedback partner and potentially a structured pilot partner;
- per-referral cash/commission is not part of the current preferred model and any remuneration structure requires legal/deontological review.

The early commission concept is therefore `SUPERSEDED`.

## 6. Data-commercialisation evolution

The June 2 AudéLor email explicitly contemplated data use/sales with owner consent.

Current authority is stricter:

> **Private by default. Contribution by choice.**

Research contribution, partner studies, commercial R&D and AI/model contribution are separate permission classes. Base-case data revenue remains `€0` until buyer demand, governance, legality, privacy/security, data quality and operational controls are evidenced.

The broad historical "consent = data sale possible" framing is `SUPERSEDED`.

## 7. Early market hypotheses

The May 29 note used an early target around ages 30–55, higher pet-spend households and an estimated French addressable segment. These numbers were internal planning calculations, not a current source-verified TAM/SAM/SOM authority.

Rule:

- retain them as historical hypothesis;
- do not reuse externally without fresh source verification and methodology;
- current market intelligence register governs current figures.

## 8. Product-positioning evolution

The May BMC already contained several durable ideas:

- non-medical positioning;
- confidence gating / system silence when evidence is insufficient;
- dual MAT + TAG architecture;
- shareable longitudinal trace for professionals;
- local/community layer.

But it also used older language about multi-agent emotional/behavioural analysis and a historical feature set that must not override current ELI/product boundaries.

## 9. Conclusion

`OPEN-MEM-LINEAGE-SUBSCRIPTION-001` is **CLOSED for origin/provenance**.
