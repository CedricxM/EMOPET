## 4. The unresolved pivot

Current controlled MAT architecture uses **four shielded coaxial piezoelectric cables** as distributed sensing elements.

This is not merely a vendor substitution within the six-zone PVDF design. It is a different capture architecture.

The historical corpus currently does **not** establish a sufficiently controlled decision record answering:

- exactly when the PVDF → coaxial pivot occurred;
- who approved it;
- whether the decisive reason was signal/coupling, cost, availability, mechanical integration, industrialisation, robustness or another factor;
- whether Alqio was explicitly rejected or simply superseded by later work;
- what evidence, if any, closed the earlier scenario.

### Open lineage gate

`OPEN-MEM-LINEAGE-PVDF-001`

**Required closure:** locate the original pivot discussion/source or create a retrospective founder decision record that distinguishes remembered rationale from documentary evidence.

Until closed:

> Do not invent a technical reason for abandoning PVDF.
