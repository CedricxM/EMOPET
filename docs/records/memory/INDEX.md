## Current-over-history rule

When a historical record conflicts with a current controlled Product, engineering, scientific, legal or strategy authority:

1. preserve the historical record;
2. preserve its date and maturity;
3. identify the superseding/current authority;
4. never silently blend the two;
5. reopen archaeology only when a concrete provenance/conflict question requires it.

Historical polish is not current authority.
