## 1. Decisions accepted for the working authority

### DEC-LCI-001 — Five-layer language architecture
**ACCEPTED FOR WORKING AUTHORITY**

1. ELS / Semantic Core
2. MotsPet
3. Explanation Grammar
4. Regional Companion
5. Local Context Engine

No lower layer may strengthen or override the semantic meaning of a higher layer.

### DEC-LCI-002 — MotsPet name
**ACCEPTED AS CURRENT CONTROLLED WORKING NAME**

“MotsPet” is the current name of the EMOPET human-language lexicon layer.

This does not establish trademark clearance, public launch naming or external-publication status.

### DEC-LCI-003 — Cultural differentiation
**ACCEPTED**

Regional companions may differ materially in rhythm, vocabulary, humour, locally natural explanation style, cultural references, territorial knowledge and recommendation framing.

They may not differ in:

- scientific meaning;
- truth class;
- confidence;
- quality/gate state;
- causal limits;
- consent/privacy/security meaning;
- medical boundary;
- provenance requirements.

### DEC-LCI-004 — Editorial explanation style
**ACCEPTED**

EMOPET should use a fluid explanatory mechanism built around:

> **Observe → Explain → Qualify → Connect**

This is a semantic grammar, not a rigid four-paragraph UI template.

Editorial references may inform readability/rhythm only; they are not scientific authority.

### DEC-LCI-005 — Adult, non-infantile companion voice
**ACCEPTED**

The default product voice should be warm, adult, fluid, precise, non-clinical and non-infantile.

“Fake cute” pet-tech language is rejected for evidence explanation.

### DEC-LCI-006 — Culturalisation is not literal translation
**ACCEPTED**

Regionalisation must be built from credible local knowledge, linguistic usage, geography, context and reviewed cultural material rather than superficial accent/slang substitution.
