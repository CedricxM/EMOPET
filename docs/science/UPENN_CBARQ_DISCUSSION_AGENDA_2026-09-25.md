# UPenn / C-BARQ discussion agenda — EMOPET

**Status:** `MEETING PREP / NO AGREEMENT IMPLIED`  
**Date:** 2026-09-25

Use this as a concise discussion guide, not as a claim that Penn has agreed to a collaboration.

## 1. Two outcomes EMOPET needs from the discussion

1. Clarify the **instrument/licensing authority** needed for a French digital C-BARQ implementation.
2. Get scientific feedback on using C-BARQ as an **external criterion** for a prospective longitudinal EMOPET validation study.

## 2. Instrument/licensing questions

- What is the current licensable French C-BARQ version?
- Does it correspond to the revised French wording used in the 2025 France study?
- Can it be embedded in a commercial digital product?
- Can EMOPET store item-level responses and derived subscale scores?
- What are the official scoring and missing/NA rules?
- Is repeated longitudinal administration permitted, and at what spacing?
- Are derivative visualizations allowed?
- Is there an authorized short form for repeated use?
- What publication/export restrictions apply?
- What licence model applies to product and/or research use?

## 3. Scientific validation questions

- Is C-BARQ suitable as an external criterion for construct-specific validation of longitudinal EMOPET observations?
- Which constructs would be scientifically reasonable to study first?
- Which constructs should explicitly **not** be inferred from sensors?
- What repeated-measures duration and questionnaire schedule would be defensible?
- What sample-size/replication standard would Penn expect?
- How should multi-dog households and Dog rivalry applicability be handled?
- What statistical design would best separate within-dog and between-dog effects?
- Would Penn prefer an independent holdout cohort/site?
- What would count as meaningful evidence versus exploratory association only?

## 4. Collaboration boundaries to make explicit

Ask, do not assume:

- scientific advisory role;
- protocol review;
- formal research collaboration;
- data-sharing;
- authorship/publication;
- instrument licensing;
- validation wording permitted in product communications.

Avoid any wording that implies Penn or Prof. Serpell already validates or endorses EMOPET.
