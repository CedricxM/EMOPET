# EMOPET — UPenn / C-BARQ discussion one-page overview

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status:** `MEETING PREP / NO PENN ENDORSEMENT OR AGREEMENT IMPLIED`

## What EMOPET is

EMOPET is a canine-wellbeing project built around a **MAT + TAG + application** architecture.

- **TAG**: wearable/mobile-context sensing during the dog's day.
- **MAT**: dedicated resting-context sensing surface, conditional on proving incremental value over TAG-only.
- **Application / Care**: longitudinal observations tied to context, provenance, quality/confidence and the individual dog's own reference.
- **ELI**: an interpretation architecture under uncertainty. It may abstain when evidence is insufficient.
- **Breiz**: bounded contextual companion; not scientific authority.

EMOPET is intentionally non-diagnostic and does not claim to know discrete canine emotions from sensors.

## Current stage

**2026 = foundation / preparation**, not commercial launch.

Current work includes:

- TAG/MAT engineering and prototype preparation;
- hardware test planning;
- scientific-methodology development;
- C-BARQ licensing investigation;
- ELI validation design;
- privacy/security/governance;
- regional/funding/ecosystem preparation.

**2027** is the current target period for the beginning of launch-phase activity and operational integration in Brittany, subject to readiness.

Exact pilot size, production volume, launch month, pricing and national rollout remain **TBD**.

## Scientific position today

- C-BARQ is being considered as a **guardian-reported external criterion candidate**, not ground truth of internal canine state.
- EMOPET has not validated ELI against C-BARQ.
- The 2025 French factorial C-BARQ study is relevant evidence for French psychometric context.
- The 63-item set retained in that study's EFA is **not** treated by EMOPET as an authorized short form.
- EMOPET does not currently have controlled evidence of a commercial C-BARQ licence or permission to reproduce protected item wording.
- No Penn/UPenn endorsement, collaboration or validation is claimed.

## Why we want to speak with Penn

We would like to clarify two independent areas.

### 1. Instrument / licensing authority

We need to understand:

- current licensable French version;
- digital/commercial embedding rights;
- item-level storage;
- scoring/subscale computation;
- repeated administration;
- missing/NA handling;
- derivative displays;
- short-form authority, if any;
- publication/export restrictions.

### 2. Scientific validation design

We would like feedback on whether C-BARQ can serve as an external criterion for selected, construct-specific longitudinal EMOPET observations.

Our proposed direction is:

- preregistered hypotheses;
- exact feature/version provenance;
- within-dog + between-dog modelling;
- explicit missingness/applicability;
- uncertainty/abstention;
- separation of training/tuning from validation;
- independent holdout/replication where feasible;
- no single global “ELI vs questionnaire” correlation presented as validation.

## What we are not asking Penn to endorse

At this stage we are **not** asking Penn to validate:

- the complete EMOPET product;
- ELI as a whole;
- any medical claim;
- any discrete-emotion classifier;
- MAT/TAG hardware performance;
- any commercial claim.

The immediate objective is to clarify instrument authority and improve the scientific design before execution.
