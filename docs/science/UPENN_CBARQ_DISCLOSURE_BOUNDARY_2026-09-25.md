# Penn / C-BARQ discussion — disclosure and claims boundary

**Prepared:** 2026-09-25  
**Status:** `CONTROLLED MEETING BOUNDARY`

## Safe wording

Use:

- “we are evaluating”;
- “we propose”;
- “candidate external criterion”;
- “subject to licence and scientific review”;
- “pre-commercial / pre-incorporation”;
- “reference/normative data access is an open question”.

## Avoid

Do not say:

- “Penn partnership”;
- “Penn-validated ELI”;
- “Professor Serpell endorses EMOPET”;
- “C-BARQ data trains our model”;
- “validated behavioural AI”;
- “licensed C-BARQ” before execution;
- “Penn data access” before a written right exists.

## Safe technical disclosure

Appropriate for this licensing call:

- non-medical product purpose;
- high-level MAT/TAG roles;
- individual-baseline and longitudinal philosophy;
- intended C-BARQ role;
- prospective study concept;
- digital administration questions;
- staged territory/users/timeline.

## Restricted unless specifically necessary

- full schematics / PCB layouts;
- source code;
- unpublished detailed ELI coefficients;
- production security/cryptographic design;
- supplier-confidential pricing or correspondence;
- unreleased CAD;
- personal/participant data;
- credentials, private keys or secrets.

## Rights boundary

A copyright licence to the questionnaire must not be assumed to include:

- normative/reference datasets;
- raw study datasets;
- research collaboration;
- model-training rights;
- sublicensing;
- publication rights;
- derivative short-form creation.

Each requires explicit written authority if relevant.
