## 4. Critical correction for the call

Do **not** describe the objective as:

> “using the C-BARQ database to train the behavioural model.”

That shortcut is broader than the controlled project direction and broader than the rights Penn has confirmed.

Current controlled position:

- C-BARQ is a **candidate external criterion / Guardian-reported behavioural instrument**;
- it is not ground truth of internal canine state;
- normative/reference-data access is an open question, not an entitlement;
- model-training/calibration rights are not assumed;
- any future ELI validation is prospective, construct-specific and independently evaluated;
- if Penn offers reference data, research data or model-development rights, those rights must be explicit.

## 5. EMOPET position to present

### Product

EMOPET is a non-medical canine observation system combining:

- TAG wearable sensing;
- MAT resting-context sensing;
- Guardian-provided context;
- a longitudinal inference layer designed to abstain when evidence is insufficient.

### Current maturity

Use restrained wording:

- hardware: Phase-0 engineering/prototype preparation;
- software: active pre-production development;
- ELI: scientifically unvalidated as a complete product;
- C-BARQ: not integrated and not licensed;
- Penn relationship: licensing/scientific discussion only.

### Current doctrine

EMOPET does not aim to:

- diagnose disease;
- replace veterinary assessment;
- classify discrete emotions directly from sensors;
- treat owner questionnaire responses as biological ground truth.
