EMOPET is a non-medical canine observation system combining:

- TAG wearable sensing;
- MAT resting-context sensing;
- Guardian-provided context;
- a longitudinal inference layer designed to abstain when evidence is insufficient.

### Current maturity

Use restrained wording:

- hardware: Phase-0 engineering/prototype preparation;
- software: active pre-production development;
- ELI: scientifically unvalidated as a complete product;
- C-BARQ: not integrated, not licensed, no protected questionnaire wording stored in the repository;
- Penn relationship: licensing/scientific discussion only.

### Current Product doctrine

EMOPET does not aim to:

- diagnose disease;
- replace veterinary assessment;
- classify discrete emotions from sensor signals;
- treat a questionnaire as direct ground truth of internal state.

The intended research use is construct-specific and longitudinal.
