# EMOPET Competitive Landscape

Status: `CONTROLLED WATCH / NOT MARKETING CLAIM AUTHORITY`

Last reviewed: `2026-08-31`

This document records public competitor evidence and the product-positioning consequences for EMOPET. It must distinguish verified public facts from EMOPET interpretation. Competitor claims are not evidence that the claimed performance is clinically or technically validated.

## Positioning rule

EMOPET must not build its primary differentiation around generic claims that competitors can also credibly make, such as:

- behaviour-change detection;
- early warning / early change awareness;
- personalised baselines;
- AI-assisted canine monitoring;
- preparing a veterinary visit;
- sharing summaries with a veterinarian.

EMOPET's stronger defensible territory is the **system architecture and evidence contract**:

- MAT + TAG rather than wearable-only observation;
- multiple sensing contexts, including off-body / resting-context observation through MAT;
- explicit signal provenance;
- quality/confidence gating;
- Valence–Arousal used descriptively rather than a generic health score;
- abstention when evidence is insufficient;
- separation between canine observation (ELI) and external territorial/cultural context (Breiz);
- a living-world/exploration experience rather than a health-dashboard-only proposition.

## Senani

Competitive relevance: `HIGH — PRE-LAUNCH / WATCH CLOSELY`

Public source review:

- https://senani.de/en/
- https://senani.de/en/facts/

### Verified public facts as of 2026-08-31

Senani publicly describes itself as a German AI-supported health tracker for dogs. Its disclosed architecture is a wearable attached to a collar or harness and based on IMU movement sensing.

Publicly stated product/development facts include:

- third tracker/app test version currently used outdoors with real dogs;
- more than 219 dog owners involved in the development phase;
- continuous movement-data capture through the wearable;
- embedded AI recognising **11+** health-relevant behaviour categories;
- analysis personalised using the individual dog's history plus characteristics such as breed, size, age and weight;
- collaboration/validation network involving veterinarians, veterinary practices, dog trainers and canine physiotherapists;
- planned veterinary integration/data sharing;
- explicit statement that Senani does **not** replace a veterinarian and does not provide the veterinary diagnosis itself;
- Senani states that it is **not a GPS tracker**;
- pre-sales are publicly scheduled to start in **2027**.

The public product site also uses a wellbeing/health-style numeric presentation. EMOPET should not copy that interface convention merely because it is familiar in the category.

### Architecture assessment

Classification: `PUBLIC-MATERIALS ASSESSMENT`, not a claim about undisclosed Senani technology.

Based on the reviewed public materials, Senani's disclosed sensing proposition is principally **wearable IMU → behaviour recognition → health-relevant change interpretation**. No ambient/off-body sensing layer equivalent to the EMOPET MAT is disclosed on the reviewed pages.

That distinction matters, but EMOPET must not overstate it. The safe statement is:

> Senani publicly discloses a collar/harness wearable using movement sensing. EMOPET's target architecture combines a wearable TAG with a separate MAT sensing context.

Do not claim that Senani "cannot" observe something unless independently demonstrated.

### Territory overlap with EMOPET

| Territory | Senani public position | EMOPET implication |
|---|---|---|
| Behaviour recognition | 11+ behaviour categories from wearable movement | not a differentiator by itself |
| Change detection | individual baseline / health-relevant behavioural changes | not a differentiator by itself |
| Personalisation | breed, size, age, weight + individual history | not a differentiator by itself |
| Early awareness | detect relevant changes before obvious symptoms | avoid generic "early warning" positioning |
| Vet preparation | summaries / future vet integration | not a differentiator by itself |
| Wearable sensing | collar/harness IMU | EMOPET should explain TAG + MAT multi-context architecture |
| GPS | explicitly not a GPS tracker | do not position against Senani primarily on GPS unless GPS is strategically relevant to EMOPET |
| Numeric wellbeing/health presentation | visible wellbeing-style score on public product UI | reinforces EMOPET choice to avoid an unsupported generic health score |
| Diagnosis boundary | says it does not replace veterinary diagnosis | EMOPET needs a stronger evidence/provenance/abstention story, not merely the same disclaimer |

### EMOPET response

**Do now**

1. Keep the MVP/demo free of a generic `health score`.
2. Make provenance visible: which signal/context supports which observation.
3. Demonstrate abstention as a first-class product behaviour.
4. Explain MAT + TAG as two complementary observation contexts, not as "more sensors = better".
5. Keep Breiz/cultural-territorial intelligence outside canine-state inference.
6. Use veterinary-preparation language only as a possible downstream workflow, not as the core differentiator.

**Do not do**

- race Senani into stronger medical/diagnostic claims;
- imply that behaviour recognition alone is proprietary territory;
- claim clinical superiority without evidence;
- invent negative claims about Senani's undisclosed capabilities;
- collapse EMOPET into a wearable health tracker category when the architecture and experience are broader.
