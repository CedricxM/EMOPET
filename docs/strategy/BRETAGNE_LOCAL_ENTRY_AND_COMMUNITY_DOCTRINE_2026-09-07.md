# EMOPET — Brittany Entry & Local Community Doctrine

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `WORKING_STRATEGY + PROJECT_DECISION ON 2027 START`

## 1. Regional-entry principle

EMOPET should prove its first market locally rather than treat national rollout as the first proof point.

Current planning authority:

- 2026 = preparation / foundation;
- 2027 = beginning of the launch phase and operational integration into the Brittany ecosystem.

Brittany is not only a sales geography. It is the first ecosystem in which EMOPET intends to learn, build credibility, create density and develop durable relationships around the product.

## 2. Rollout ladder

The working regional rollout architecture is:

- **L0 — Global Core:** product/scientific/privacy fundamentals that must remain coherent everywhere;
- **L1 — Territory Seed:** initial local content, actors, places and context sufficient to enter a territory without pretending the local network is already mature;
- **L2 — Verified Local:** locally relevant information/partners/places that have been verified and can be represented with stronger confidence;
- **L3 — Activated Local Network:** real operational relationships, recurring local activity and a sufficiently dense ecosystem to create durable local value.

This ladder is a working strategy, not evidence that each level is already achieved.

## 3. Lorient as proof environment

Lorient is the practical starting point for local proof and ecosystem learning.

The project should use the region to test:

- Guardian understanding and willingness to pay;
- onboarding and longitudinal value;
- veterinary usefulness;
- local discovery/community utility;
- support burden;
- partner interest;
- product trust;
- field reliability.

Exact pilot sizes remain controlled hypotheses unless separately approved/evidenced.

## 4. Community doctrine

Community exists to return people to real local life, not to create a generic follower/feed product.

Core verbs:

- **MEET** — meet locally;
- **HELP** — mutual aid;
- **LEARN** — transmit knowledge;
- **SHARE** — share useful/local experience.

The digital layer facilitates connection. The intended value is realised in the real local world.

## 5. Local actor model

Potential local-network actors may include, when relationships are real and appropriately represented:

- veterinarians and professionals;
- associations;
- shelters;
- community/youth centres;
- libraries / local public-interest venues;
- dog-friendly places;
- relevant local businesses;
- regional research/education actors.

No local entity should be described as a partner without evidence of that relationship.

## 6. Breiz and regional adaptation

Breiz is the contextual companion layer that can help EMOPET adapt to a territory without changing the scientific core.

Regionalisation may affect:

- local culture/context;
- places;
- events;
- useful seasonal information;
- local recommendations;
- language/tone where appropriate.

Regionalisation must not change scientific, privacy, consent or safety meaning.

## 7. Scale rule

> **Scale region by region, after evidence.**

Regional expansion should follow demonstrated product value and local-operational learning, rather than assume that a national launch automatically creates community density.

## 8. Source anchors

This working doctrine consolidates prior project presentations, local-market strategy, Community doctrine, current Brittany product prototypes and the controlled 2026–2027 project timeline.
