# EMOPET — Canonical Project Timeline 2026–2027

**Decision date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `PROJECT_DECISION / PLANNING AUTHORITY`

## Canonical correction

EMOPET does **not** treat 2026 as its commercial launch year.

The current project decision is:

- **2026 = foundation and preparation period**;
- **2027 = beginning of the launch phase and the period in which EMOPET starts integrating operationally into the Brittany ecosystem**.

This file is the planning authority for future strategy notes, decks and AI-assisted project work unless a later controlled decision supersedes it.

## 2026 — foundation / preparation

2026 is used to build the conditions required for a credible 2027 launch phase, including as applicable:

- product and prototype development;
- MAT/TAG industrial preparation;
- supplier discussions and RFQ work;
- scientific methodology and research exchanges;
- C-BARQ licensing investigation;
- regulatory/compliance preparation;
- legal and IP structuring;
- team legal/confidentiality instruments;
- funding and regional-support discussions;
- veterinary-channel design;
- competitor and market intelligence;
- evidence architecture and repository governance;
- relationship building with relevant ecosystem actors.

Work performed in 2026 may be externally visible, tested or discussed, but that does not make 2026 the commercial launch year.

## 2027 — launch phase + Brittany integration

2027 is the target period to begin:

- operational launch activity;
- real-world product introduction according to readiness;
- regional ecosystem integration in Brittany;
- structured early-user / field learning;
- progressive veterinary and professional-network integration where ready;
- local credibility and relationship building around the project;
- conversion of 2026 preparation work into operational pilots, commercial activity or formal collaborations when evidence/readiness permits.

The strategic intent is not simply "sell in Brittany". Brittany is the first ecosystem in which EMOPET intends to begin embedding itself operationally, learning locally and building durable project presence.

## What is not fixed by this decision

This record does **not** yet fix:

- exact launch month or quarter;
- exact commercial geography beyond the Brittany integration priority;
- production volume;
- pilot size;
- revenue target;
- national rollout date;
- specific partnership status;
- funding award;
- final pricing/subscription structure.

Those items require separate controlled decisions or evidence.

## Conflict rule

If another repository file, deck or AI-generated summary implies that EMOPET's commercial launch is planned for 2026, treat that statement as outdated unless it is explicitly about preparation, prototype work, pre-launch testing or relationship-building.

Future planning content should reference this file rather than infer launch timing from document dates.

## Revision rule

If the launch calendar changes, do not silently overwrite this decision. Create a dated replacement decision, mark this record `SUPERSEDED`, and document the reason for the change.
