# EMOPET — Industrial & Funding Governance

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `PROJECT_DECISION / GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY`

## 1. Industrial authority principle

MOKO or another supplier may be asked to own clearly scoped engineering work, including where agreed:

- detailed bench design;
- fabrication/assembly;
- instrumentation;
- test execution;
- raw acquisition;
- engineering analysis;
- DFM/DFA observations;
- sourcing recommendations;
- manufacturability risk identification;
- prototype quotation.

That does **not** transfer EMOPET's product/design authority.

Supplier recommendations must remain recommendations until an explicit EMOPET decision promotes them.

## 2. No silent design promotion

The following events do not, by themselves, freeze the design:

- supplier recommendation;
- supplier silence;
- quote issuance;
- DFM comment;
- prototype fabrication;
- document marked prepared/approved in an older historical pack.

Any architecture change, freeze, supplier selection, production release, tooling or NPI authorization requires a separately recorded EMOPET decision.

## 3. Phase 0 is an evidence programme

Phase 0 exists to reduce uncertainty.

Negative, inconclusive or iterate outcomes are valid engineering results.

Phase 0 must not be sold internally or externally as proof that the final product is production-ready.

Supplier evidence should return enough detail to reproduce and audit the result, including raw data, as-tested configuration, deviations, failed/aborted runs when recoverable and explicit GO / ITERATE / NO-GO recommendations separated from EMOPET approval.

## 4. Irreversible-spend gate

EMOPET does not progress to major irreversible production expenditure simply because documentation is complete.

The next spend is authorised when the combination of evidence, cost, quality, risk and responsibility supports it.

This principle applies especially to:

- production PCB release;
- tooling;
- major NRE;
- certification commitments;
- first-series production;
- irreversible supplier lock-in.

## 5. Funding doctrine

Public support should primarily **buy proof, de-risking and external expertise**, not decorate a business plan.

Priority use cases for funding/support include, where eligible:

- Phase 0 engineering and sensing proof;
- independent electronics review;
- scientific review / validation design;
- FTO / IP work;
- regulatory/compliance expertise;
- prototype and pre-compliance work;
- other external work packages that close a material evidence gap.

## 6. AudéLor / Emergys gate

AudéLor / Emergys is a structuring gate before major prototype/industrial spend, subject to actual eligibility and timing rules.

Never treat:

- aid ceilings as awarded money;
- candidate expenditure as eligible expenditure;
- a programme description as confirmation of EMOPET eligibility;
- an application pathway as secured financing.

Exact eligibility, dates, intervention rates, geography and eligible supplier rules must be evidenced by current programme/contact information.

## 7. 2026 / 2027 sequencing

2026 is used to prepare the evidence chain and funding architecture.

2027 begins the launch phase and operational integration in Brittany. Funding and industrial work should therefore be sequenced to convert 2026 preparation into credible 2027 field activity rather than force premature production.
