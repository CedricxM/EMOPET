# EMOPET — Core Capability & Activation Doctrine

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Recovered founder decision date:** 2026-08-12  
**Status:** `PROJECT_DECISION / FOUNDER LOCK — CHAT-RECOVERED`  
**Purpose:** preserve the distinction between product identity, implementation maturity and activation timing.

## 1. Three concepts must never be collapsed

For EMOPET, these are separate questions:

1. **Is the capability part of the intended EMOPET product identity?**
2. **Is the capability implemented / tested / production-ready?**
3. **Is the capability activated for a given launch stage, geography or user cohort?**

A capability may be strategically CORE while remaining dormant, gated, partially implemented or unavailable in a given release.

Conversely, a prototype or implemented component is not automatically a core launch commitment.

## 2. Community and World are core EMOPET capabilities

Founder decision recovered from 2026-08-12:

- **Community is part of the core EMOPET experience.**
- **World is part of the core EMOPET experience.**
- Neither should be reclassified as a disposable future concept merely because implementation, safety, density or launch gates remain open.
- These capabilities are not to be silently traded away in architecture simplification exercises.

The public explanation may still simplify EMOPET as **MAT + TAG + application**, but that public facade does not erase the internal product identity.

## 3. Activation is evidence- and density-gated

Community and World may require activation thresholds such as:

- sufficient active-user density;
- moderation readiness;
- privacy/safety readiness;
- regional content/local-network readiness;
- technical/runtime readiness;
- abuse/adversarial QA;
- explicit founder/product release decision.

A gate delays activation. It does not by itself remove the capability from the product architecture.

## 4. Immersive experience at launch

Founder direction recovered from 2026-08-12:

> The immersive dimension is intended to exist from the beginning of the operational EMOPET experience; what may vary is the **breadth and quantity of experiences**, not whether immersive capability belongs to the product at all.

This is a launch/product-direction decision, not evidence that a Unity/Nakama runtime is currently implemented or production-ready.

The exact 2027 immersive launch slice remains to be defined and tested against technical, safety, privacy and product gates.

## 5. Zero One / first immersive concept

`Zero One` was retained in prior project discussion as an **initial candidate concept** for the immersive experience.

Status:

`CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENTLY RELEASED / REQUIRES RE-EVALUATION`

Do not present Zero One as a released product, but do not erase its lineage from the project merely because the implementation is not current.

## 6. Unity / Nakama handling

Unity and Nakama remain architectural/runtime candidates associated with the immersive/social workstream.

Rules:

- do not archive or remove them solely because the current product is not production-ready;
- do not claim they are active production services without runtime evidence;
- keep Unity as presentation/client-side immersive technology unless a later authority changes that choice;
- Nakama, if used, must not become durable authority for Guardian identity, consent, ELI truth, balances/entitlements or other durable product truth that belongs in controlled backend data stores;
- any final runtime selection remains separately gated.

## 7. Launch-scope matrix rule

Every future launch plan should show at least two axes:

| Capability | Product identity | Activation / maturity |
|---|---|---|
| MAT | CORE | status from engineering/release authority |
| TAG | CORE | status from engineering/release authority |
| Care / application | CORE | status from software/product authority |
| Breiz | CORE / bounded | activation subject to semantic/AI gates |
| Community | CORE | activation/density/safety gated |
| World / immersive | CORE | launch slice + runtime/safety gated |
| Memories / Together / continuity surfaces | CORE or progressive according to their domain authority | exact launch activation separately decided |

Do not use `FUTURE CONCEPT` as a synonym for `NOT YET IMPLEMENTED`.
