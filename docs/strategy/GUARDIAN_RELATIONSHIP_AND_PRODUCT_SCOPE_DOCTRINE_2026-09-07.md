# EMOPET — Guardian Relationship & Product Scope Doctrine

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `PROJECT_DECISION / FOUNDER LOCK`  
**Origin:** consolidation of accepted project decisions from 2026-08-06 onward; does not promote unimplemented capabilities to released status.

## 1. Unique relationship authority

EMOPET must centralise person–dog authority around one coherent relationship domain:

`GUARDIAN_DOG_RELATIONSHIP`

The Guardian relationship is the authority boundary for, where applicable:

- dog profile and household relationship;
- MAT/TAG ownership and device binding;
- data and longitudinal history;
- permissions and consents;
- activities and Together suggestions;
- Moments and Memories;
- visibility and social sharing;
- veterinary/professional sharing;
- household, pet-sitter or trusted-person access;
- export, revocation and deletion.

These rights must not be fragmented into unrelated feature-specific permission systems.

## 2. Nothing becomes social automatically

No Care observation, MAT/TAG signal, ELI inference, Guardian journal entry, private Memory, precise location, veterinary information or raw Breiz conversation becomes public/social by default.

Every transition into a social/professional surface requires the appropriate explicit audience/purpose authority.

## 3. Relationship-first, never relationship-scoring

EMOPET exists to support the lived relationship, not to optimise or rank the dog or Guardian.

Forbidden product directions include:

- relationship score;
- “good owner” score;
- public relationship comparison;
- physiological competition;
- dog-performance leaderboard;
- algorithmic guarantee of compatibility;
- using product engagement as proof of a better relationship.

## 4. Together doctrine

Together follows the relationship loop:

`UNDERSTAND → SUGGEST → LIVE → LEARN → REMEMBER`

Activities are suggestions, not prescriptions.

Ranking must apply eligibility/safety/constraints before preference ranking. Recommendations may use permitted dog, Guardian and contextual information but must preserve uncertainty and user choice.

The system should distinguish activities that are:

- suggested by EMOPET;
- discovered locally;
- hosted/organised;
- professional.

No activity suggestion becomes a medical, behavioural or relationship prescription.

## 5. Public product facade vs internal capability map

For simple external explanation, EMOPET may be presented as:

> **MAT + TAG + application**

That simplification must not erase the internal ecosystem architecture.

Together, Help/Learn/Share, Moments, Memories, Community, Relationship Engine, Local/Regional Culture, Trust/consent ledgers, ELS/MotsPet, Breiz and World are capabilities/domains within the EMOPET ecosystem, not automatically separate consumer products.

## 6. Capability status discipline

Use activation/maturity language without confusing product importance with launch state.

A capability may be strategically core while remaining dormant, progressive, activation-gated or unimplemented.

Relevant status concepts include:

- `CORE` — belongs to the intended product architecture;
- `LAUNCH-ACTIVE` — intended to be active in a specific launch scope, subject to readiness;
- `PROGRESSIVE` — deliberately introduced in stages;
- `ACTIVATION-GATED` / `GATED` — product capability retained in architecture but not active until defined gates close;
- `EXPERIMENTAL` — requires evidence before promotion;
- `NOT IMPLEMENTED` — documented intent only.

Do not archive a core capability merely because it is gated. Do not call a gated capability released merely because it is documented.

## 7. Community / World relationship

Community and World remain distinct but connected surfaces.

- Community: groups, events, discoveries, controlled publication and local interaction.
- World: optional interactive persistent social/shared experience.
- Optional real-world Meet: separate consent, location and safety gates.

No automatic publication of Care/ELI/private data occurs across these boundaries.

## 8. Local/privacy constraints

Social/local experiences should use the minimum location precision necessary.

- broad/local context may be used when appropriate;
- exact location disclosure requires an explicit, purpose-specific transition;
- blocking, reporting, moderation, visibility rules and harassment/abuse controls are architectural requirements, not post-launch decoration.
