## 1. Product architecture lock

EMOPET is not a single wearable and not a questionnaire product.

The core product architecture is:

- **MAT + TAG + application**;
- MAT provides a dedicated rest-context sensing surface;
- TAG provides mobile/context sensing during the dog's day;
- the application builds longitudinal understanding around the individual dog's own reference;
- ELI interprets under uncertainty and may abstain;
- Breiz is a bounded contextual companion, not the scientific authority of the system.

The strategic value is the combined evidence chain, not the number of sensors.

## 2. MAT product principle

MAT must remain a **real resting object / bed first**, not become a laboratory instrument disguised as a consumer object.

The hardware must preserve ordinary domestic use while creating a reliable qualified rest context.

A more complex sensing stack is not automatically better. Hardware complexity must earn its place through evidence.

## 3. Relationship-first positioning

EMOPET is relationship-first rather than metric-first.

The product is intended to help the Guardian understand changes in the dog's lived pattern over time, not to flood the interface with generic scores.

The product experience should favour:

- longitudinal context;
- memory;
- continuity;
- provenance;
- qualified uncertainty;
- useful prompts;
- local human connection.

## 4. ELI doctrine

ELI is an interpretation architecture under uncertainty. It must not be presented as an engine that knows a discrete emotion with certainty.

Core rules:

- confidence matters;
- provenance matters;
- quality gates matter;
- insufficient information may produce no output;
- no medical diagnosis;
- no unsupported generic emotional score;
- model sophistication is not evidence of scientific validation.

The ability to abstain is a product behaviour, not a failure state.

## 5. Breiz doctrine

Breiz is a transparent, bounded contextual AI companion.

Breiz may:

- explain qualified trends in accessible language;
- retrieve validated/controlled information;
- use permitted preferences and regional context;
- suggest activities, places, events or contextual actions.

Breiz must not:

- diagnose;
- convert raw data directly into unsupported claims;
- become the scientific source of truth;
- act socially or spatially on the user's behalf without the required confirmation/permission.

Breiz is part of the experience, not the centre of scientific authority.

## 6. Differentiation doctrine

EMOPET should not claim that generic behaviours such as AI monitoring, behaviour recognition, personalised baselines, early-change awareness or veterinary summaries are unique by themselves.

The stronger strategic territory is the **evidence contract**:

- MAT + TAG multi-context architecture;
- explicit provenance;
- quality/confidence gating;
- abstention;
- individual longitudinal reference;
- careful separation between observation and diagnosis;
- local/living-world experience layered above the scientific core.

## 7. Trust is part of the product

Privacy, consent, export/delete rights, sharing controls and abstention are not peripheral legal features. They are part of the product proposition.

The project should charge for additional value, not for fundamental user control or trust rights.

## 8. Maturity discipline

A document, architecture, model, supplier recommendation or prototype target must never be silently promoted into a validated result.

Use explicit maturity states and preserve distinctions between:

- decided;
- implemented;
- tested;
- validated;
- proposed/candidate;
- open;
- blocked;
- historical/superseded.
