# EMOPET — Scientific Commons & Governance Direction

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `FOUNDER PRINCIPLE / GOVERNANCE DIRECTION — NOT YET FORMALISED`

## 1. Scientific openness principle

EMOPET intends to distinguish between what should remain protected engineering know-how and what should remain scientifically examinable.

Current founder direction:

- scientific findings produced from EMOPET research should be publishable rather than retained solely as competitive advantage;
- appropriately governed data should be capable of supporting independent scientific research;
- scientific credit should go to the people/institutions that perform the scientific work;
- scientific claims should remain open to criticism and examination by qualified researchers;
- engineering, sensing architecture, inference implementation, industrial know-how and other protectable technical assets remain subject to IP/confidentiality strategy.

This is a governance direction, not evidence that an open-data programme, publication agreement or institutional governance body already exists.

## 2. Separation of authority

Commercial convenience must not silently override scientific method.

The intended governance separation is:

- **EMOPET product/engineering authority:** implementation, sensing architecture, security, manufacturing, product experience and protected engineering decisions;
- **scientific authority:** claims, methodology, validation design, interpretation boundaries and publication standards should be reviewed/governed with qualified scientific institutions or experts as formal relationships develop.

The exact institutional mechanism remains to be designed.

## 3. Claims doctrine

EMOPET should prefer a narrower scientifically defensible claim over a broader commercially attractive claim that has not been validated.

Important permanent boundaries:

- observation is not diagnosis;
- model sophistication is not validation;
- longitudinal correlation is not causal proof;
- a scientific conversation is not endorsement;
- a licensed questionnaire is not validation of EMOPET's inference layer;
- external scientific advice must be attributed at the level actually evidenced.

## 4. Serpell / C-BARQ consequence

The current Serpell/UPenn workstream must preserve the distinction between:

- EMOPET proposal;
- Prof. Serpell feedback/recommendation;
- EMOPET adoption of a recommendation;
- C-BARQ commercial licensing;
- institutional partnership;
- scientific validation of EMOPET/ELI.

## 5. Complete C-BARQ direction

The historical reduced/subset direction is superseded.

Current direction is to investigate licensing and scientifically defensible integration of the **complete validated C-BARQ instrument**, with UX/pacing designed around the science rather than silently shortening the instrument for convenience.

Any idea of distributing questions over time remains a design proposal until instrument owners/scientific experts confirm that the administration conditions preserve validity.

## 6. Longitudinal research value

EMOPET may eventually produce longitudinal observations from real domestic conditions at scale and duration that are difficult to reproduce in controlled studies.

That potential creates responsibilities:

- clear consent/governance;
- high-quality provenance;
- documented missingness and device/model versions;
- reproducible methods;
- privacy protection;
- controlled access;
- publication/credit rules;
- no overclaiming from observational data.

## 7. Commercial-data boundary

Scientific openness does not imply indiscriminate data commercialisation.

Research contribution, partner studies, commercial R&D and AI/model contribution remain distinct permission classes under the canonical data doctrine:

> **Private by default. Contribution by choice.**

## 8. Next formalisation gates

This governance direction should eventually be converted into formal instruments, potentially including:

- scientific advisory/governance charter;
- publication policy;
- data-access/research governance policy;
- authorship/credit principles;
- conflict-of-interest rules;
- scientific-claims approval workflow;
- institutional collaboration agreements where appropriate.

Until those exist, describe this as EMOPET's intended governance direction, not as an established institutional governance structure.
