# EMOPET — Data, Trust & Business Model Doctrine

**Control date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `PROJECT_DECISION / BUSINESS + DATA GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY`

## 1. Canonical doctrine

> **Private by default. Contribution by choice.**

EMOPET must not treat household/dog data as an automatically monetisable asset.

Every contribution beyond private product use must be purpose-specific, understandable, traceable and revocable where applicable.

## 2. No global “sell my data” switch

Contribution modes remain distinct. Current strategic categories include:

1. private use;
2. research contribution;
3. partner studies;
4. commercial R&D contribution;
5. separate AI/model contribution.

One permission must not silently authorize another purpose.

## 3. Minimum contribution disclosure

A contribution request should disclose, where relevant:

- who is requesting access;
- purpose;
- data categories;
- duration;
- anonymised/pseudonymised form;
- AI/model-training use;
- location use;
- EU / non-EU transfer;
- retention;
- downstream sharing;
- Guardian benefit or compensation;
- whether EMOPET is remunerated;
- withdrawal route;
- contact / governance route.

## 4. Data-revenue financial rule

**Base-case data revenue = €0 until demand is evidenced.**

No forecast may recognise data revenue merely because a possible data product has been designed.

Before revenue is recognised, the project should have evidence covering at least:

- sufficient real data;
- data quality;
- participant willingness;
- buyer willingness-to-pay;
- lawful governance / contracts;
- security and privacy controls;
- a clearly defined product rather than raw uncontrolled data dumps.

## 5. Approved direction for knowledge products

Potential future outputs may include governed cohort access, sponsored studies, aggregated benchmarks, controlled APIs or datasets for evaluation/training when explicitly authorised.

Strategic principle:

> **Sell governed knowledge, not uncontrolled raw data dumps.**

Universities, veterinary schools, research laboratories and legitimate animal-health / pet-tech R&D may become relevant counterparties subject to governance.

Data brokers and behavioural-advertising resale are not approved buyer classes.

## 6. Data Quality Passport

Any future research/commercial dataset should document at least:

- signal/source origin;
- relevant hardware/firmware version;
- calibration;
- missingness;
- quality state;
- provenance;
- model/version lineage;
- cohort definition;
- rights/permissions;
- retention;
- privacy protection method.

## 7. Trust rights are not Premium features

Fundamental rights and safety controls must remain independent of subscription state.

Do not paywall:

- privacy controls;
- consent withdrawal;
- deletion/export rights;
- block/report and essential safety controls;
- access to already-created private memories where applicable.

Premium may enrich interpretation, history, reports or professional sharing, but it must not purchase basic control over one's own data.

## 8. Raw audio boundary

The product doctrine is that TAG audio-related sensing should reduce the modality to derived/event/contextual features rather than retain/transmit raw household audio.

This remains a control objective requiring technical verification. A documented rule is not implementation proof.

## 9. Professional sharing

Vet/partner sharing should be bound to:

- purpose;
- recipient;
- scope;
- time period;
- expiry;
- revocation;
- audit/logging where applicable.

No professional receives permanent access to raw/private household history by default.

## 10. Current economic posture

EMOPET's financial model is layered and still requires evidence.

- Hardware price and COGS remain open until quoted/proven.
- Subscription pricing remains a hypothesis until willingness-to-pay and retention are tested.
- Community/basic trust surfaces remain freemium where defined by current controlled doctrine.
- Data revenue remains upside-gated, not a rescue mechanism for weak hardware economics.
